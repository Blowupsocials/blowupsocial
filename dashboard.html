Frequently Asked Questions
How long does delivery take?

Most orders start within minutes and complete within 1–24 hours depending on the service.

Is my account safe?

Yes. We only need your public profile URL — never your username or password.

What is the refill policy?

Services marked "Refill" are eligible for a free top-up if numbers drop within 30 days. Contact support to request.

How do I add funds?

Click "Add Funds" anywhere on the dashboard. We accept Bank Transfer. Minimum deposit is ₦3,000.
