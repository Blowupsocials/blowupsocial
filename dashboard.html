Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about BlowUpSocials
How long does delivery take?
Most orders start within minutes and complete within 1—24 hours. Some high-quality or geo-targeted services may take up to 72 hours. You can track progress in My Orders.
Is my account safe to use these services?
Yes. We only need your public profile URL — never your password or login details. Our delivery methods are gradual and organic-looking to keep your account safe.
What happens if my numbers drop after delivery?
Services labelled Refill include a free top-up guarantee if counts drop within 30 days of completion. Email support with your order ID to request a refill.
How do I fund my wallet?
Click Add Funds anywhere on the dashboard. We accept Bank Transfer via Korapay. Minimum deposit is ₦3,000 and funds are credited instantly after payment confirmation.
Can I get a refund?
Wallet top-ups are non-refundable once funds are credited. However, if an order fails to start within 24 hours, the charge is automatically reversed to your wallet balance.
Which platforms do you support?
We currently support Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X (Twitter), YouTube, Snapchat, and Telegram. New platforms are added regularly — check the Social Boost page for the latest list.
